WHO WE ARE

A wear-parts factory, not a trading desk.

Crushura is a Chinese manufacturer of wear-resistant components for crushing, sand-making, screening, grinding, mixing and conveying machinery. Established in 1973, the company today operates three factories covering a total of 130,000 square meters, with more than 300 employees.

Over 50 years of manufacturing, we have built deep expertise in product quality and wear-resistant components — from material selection through casting, forging and heat treatment to final machining and testing. Our core work is making the parts that absorb the punishment, so the machines around them last longer.

Regardless of where your machinery was sourced, our technical specialists verify the exact dimensions and model numbers of the parts you need, ensuring perfect compatibility with your equipment. We manufacture — and we stand behind everything that leaves our factory.
