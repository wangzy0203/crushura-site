Crushing Machinery

Home / Crushing Machinery

Jaw, impact, cone, hammer and roll crushers — and the wear parts that take the punishment.

PE Jaw Crusher Jaw plates, cheek plates and the moving jaw for PE-series primary crushers. Every casting comes out of our own foundry.
Impact Crusher Hammer plates, counterplate liners and rotors for deep-chamber vortex and hard-rock impact crushers.
Hydraulic Cone Crusher Grinding liners, eccentric shaft sleeves and hydraulic parts for multi-cylinder and single-cylinder cone crushers.
Hammer Crusher Crusher hammers, grate bars and grate plates for standard and heavy-duty hammer crushers. Hammer material chosen for the rock you crush.
Double Roll Crusher Toothed rollers and roll bearing assemblies for double roll crushers in mining, chemical and building-materials lines.
