APPLICATIONS

Built for your industry.

Home / Applications

Every industry wears parts differently. Tell us your material and duty cycle — we configure alloys and apertures to match, not the other way around.

Industry 01

Mining & Aggregates

Hard rock is where wear parts earn their keep. Our jaw plates, blow bars, hammers and screen meshes are cast from high-manganese and high-chromium alloys matched to your rock hardness and duty cycle. From primary crushing to final screening, we keep quarry lines running longer between changeouts.

Industry 02

Cement & Building Materials

Limestone, clinker and raw meal punish liners and grinding media day after day. We supply abrasion-resistant castings for crushers, ball mills and vertical mills, plus the conveyor components that move material between them. Material grades are selected against your abrasiveness index, not a generic catalogue.

Industry 03

Chemical & Fertiliser

Granules and powders demand clean screening and enclosed conveying. Our screen meshes, feeders, conveyor belts and elevator chains are configured for corrosive or sticky materials, with polyurethane and stainless options where the process calls for them.

Industry 04

Food & Pharmaceutical

Screening and conveying in food-grade plants leaves no room for contamination. We manufacture meshes, blades and conveyor components with food-safe material options and smooth, cleanable surfaces, and verify dimensions against your existing machines before anything ships.

Industry 05

Power & Metallurgy

Coal, slag and ore handling never sleeps. Our hammers, liners, belts and idlers are built for continuous duty in power stations and steel works, where one unplanned stop costs more than a year of spare parts. Stock programs keep critical wear parts ready before they are needed.

NOT ON THE LIST?

Your material decides the part, not our catalogue.

If your industry is not listed, send us the machine model and the material you run. We will tell you honestly whether our castings fit the job.
